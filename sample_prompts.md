# Sample Prompts by Level

## Level 1: single component
1) A 2x2 MZI.
2) A four-channel WDM.
3) Directional coupler with a 0.5um gap and 50um coupling length. 
4) A microring with coupler and heater.

## Level 2: two components with a single connecting edge
1) Two cascaded MZIs, each with TiN heaters for C-band operation. The device should have 500 nm wide waveguides and 10 um long heaters. Both MZIs have two input/output ports.
2) Connect a 2x2 MZI with heaters to a grating coupler. Port 1 to port 0.
3) Connect a 2x2 MZI with heaters to a grating coupler. Port 0 to port 0.
4) Connect a 1x2 splitter to a 1x1 microring resonator.
    
## Level 3: 3-10 components
1) Layout 1x2 MMIs connected to each other to for a 1x8 splitter tree.
2) A power splitter connected to two 1*1 MZIs with doped heaters each with a path difference 100 um. The two output ports of the splitter are the input ports of the two MZIs respectively.
3) Eight low loss and low power thermo optic phase shifters. The phase shifters should be arranged in parallel along the y direction in an array.
4) Four microrings with heaters, arranged in an array along the y direction, each connected to a grating coupler. 

## Level 4: 10+ components
1) A 1x16 splitter tree built from 1x2 MMIs.
2) A 3x4 mesh of 2x2 MZIs with back‑to‑back connections between adjacent nodes.
3) Sixteen thermo‑optic phase shifters arranged in a 4x4 mesh.
4) A 1x8 splitter tree using 1x2 MMIs, and connect each output to a grating coupler.
